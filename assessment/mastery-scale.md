# Mastery Assessment Scale

This scale records **demonstrated human capability**, not the sophistication of AI output.

| Level | Name | Demonstrated capability |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Assisted | Can request or observe a result but cannot yet explain the process |
| 1 | Recognition | Recognizes major concepts and terminology |
| 2 | Explanation | Explains the task, major decisions, and concepts |
| 3 | Reproduction | Performs the core task with limited guidance |
| 4 | Adaptation | Modifies the workflow for a changed requirement |
| 5 | Diagnosis | Detects errors, weak assumptions, or poor AI output and proposes corrections |
| 6 | Prompt Design | Builds effective prompts from acquired knowledge rather than copied formulas |
| 7 | Independent Practice | Performs and verifies without AI supplying every major step |
| 8 | Teaching | Accurately teaches or mentors another person |
| 9 | Systemization | Converts mastery into reusable standards, templates, assessments, training, or team workflows |

## Evidence Rule

Assign a level only when the capability has been demonstrated through observable work.

Untested dimensions stay explicitly **untested**.

Project completion and learning progress are separate. A finished artifact does not automatically increase mastery.

Unresolved capability gaps should be recorded as **learning debt**.
