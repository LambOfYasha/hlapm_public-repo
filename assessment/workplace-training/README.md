# Workplace Training Adaptation

Organizations should not ask only:

> **Can the employee get AI to produce polished output?**

A stronger question is:

> **Can they understand, direct, evaluate, verify, recover from, and reliably deliver the AI-assisted work required by their role?**

## Suggested Progression

`Role Requirements → Baseline Knowledge → AI-Assisted Learning → Guided Live Work → Assessment → Challenge → Independent Production → Advanced Challenge → Teaching / Leadership / Systemization`

## Role Relevance

Do not test employees on irrelevant skills. Define:

- what must be understood independently;
- what may appropriately remain AI-assisted;
- what must be independently verified;
- what requires manager or professional review.

## Example: AI-Assisted Developer

Assessment might require the learner to identify and correct an AI-generated implementation problem in a real bounded feature.

Challenge might require the learner to author the production prompt, implement the feature, test it, document it, and deliver by an explicit deadline.

## Example: AI-Assisted Content Work

Assessment might require identifying unsupported claims and correcting a real draft.

Challenge might require constructing the research/editorial prompt, producing a publishable artifact, verifying claims, documenting sources, and delivering under deadline.

## Consequential Decisions

AI-generated evaluation should not be the sole authority for consequential employment decisions. Combine objective evidence, appropriate verification, retained records, and human managerial judgment. Follow applicable employment, privacy, accessibility, and organizational requirements.
