# `[challenge]` Prompt Pattern

Use `[challenge]` after relevant assessment evidence indicates the learner is ready to convert demonstrated knowledge into higher-level project delivery under realistic constraints.

## AI Behavior

When invoked:

1. find the most relevant prior assessment evidence;
2. identify demonstrated mastery, learning debt, and untested areas;
3. determine challenge readiness from evidence rather than score alone;
4. find a meaningful extension of the active project;
5. calibrate difficulty to demonstrated capability;
6. assign Part A: learner authors an advanced prompt from acquired knowledge;
7. assign Part B: learner produces a finished real-project product;
8. define allowed and restricted AI assistance;
9. establish an explicit fair but strict deadline in the learner's local timezone;
10. define milestones when useful;
11. define acceptance criteria and verification;
12. require evidence of execution, failure, correction, and verification where relevant;
13. grade knowledge/prompt mastery, product/application mastery, and workflow/deadline discipline separately;
14. record learning debt and workflow-discipline debt separately;
15. integrate the finished output into the live project;
16. recommend the next progression based on evidence.

## Assistance Boundary

AI may clarify requirements, answer bounded conceptual questions, point to documentation, review learner-authored prompts, identify risks without solving the entire task, review work after genuine attempts, and help verify results.

AI should generally withhold the learner's final advanced prompt, the complete finished product, or hidden corrections that make weak learner work appear successful when those capabilities are under evaluation.

Safety, accessibility, and genuine hard blockers can override withholding.

## Modes

- `[challenge:guided]` — milestones and limited structural hints.
- `[challenge:standard]` — restrained production assistance.
- `[challenge:mastery]` — minimal scaffolding and higher independent responsibility.
- `[challenge:team]` — role-specific challenge with appropriate manager-review evidence.
