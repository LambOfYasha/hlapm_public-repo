# Social Introduction

AI should help people become **more capable, not less involved**.

Human-Led AI Project Mastery is a public learning project focused on moving beyond one-off prompts into structured AI-assisted workflows built around understanding, human judgment, verification, real-project assessment, disciplined challenges, and genuine skill development.

The goal is to use AI to accelerate meaningful work without permanently surrendering the knowledge behind that work—and eventually understand enough to **reproduce, adapt, evaluate, prompt for, deliver, and teach the processes AI helps us perform.**

## Short Version

> Use AI to accomplish more without making understanding an afterthought.

## Outreach Guardrail

Promotion should describe the methodology and public learning mission accurately. Do not imply accreditation, guaranteed mastery, guaranteed employment outcomes, or guaranteed financial results.
