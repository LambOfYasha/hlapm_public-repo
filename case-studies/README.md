# Case Studies

Case studies should show more than what AI produced. They should document what the human became more capable of doing through the work.

## Suggested Structure

1. Starting knowledge and constraints
2. Real project goal
3. AI assistance used
4. Human-owned decisions
5. Important failures or corrections
6. Verification method
7. Learning debt discovered
8. Assessment evidence
9. Challenge evidence, when applicable
10. Finished project result
11. Newly demonstrated human capability
12. Remaining untested areas
13. What should change in the workflow next time

Protect private information and obtain appropriate consent before publishing identifiable learner, employer, client, or organizational material.
